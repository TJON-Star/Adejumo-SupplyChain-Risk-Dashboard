# Initial prospect list and outreach plan — inventory control support

**Prepared:** 21 November 2026  
**Offer:** Inventory reconciliation and stock-control support for agro-processing, food manufacturing, palm oil, cassava, grain and commodity businesses.  
**Portfolio:** [Interactive dashboard](https://tjon-star.github.io/Adejumo-SupplyChain-Risk-Dashboard/inventory-reconciliation-demo/)

## Positioning

Do not pitch this as generic Power BI development. Pitch a bounded operational service:
- reconcile inventory ledger to physical count at an agreed cut-off;
- identify and classify stock exceptions (including negative balances and transfer mismatches);
- improve item/location master-data quality;
- maintain an action register with owners, due dates and evidence references;
- provide a management dashboard and periodic exception report.

The portfolio dashboard is explicitly simulated. Never present its fictional discrepancies or monetary values as a prospect's actual results, or as proof that the service has delivered savings.

## Outreach sequence

1. Prioritise Worths Foods, Niji Group, Matna Foods and JB Farms: relevant operating models and geography make them the strongest initial fit.
2. Contact the Operations Manager / COO / Supply Chain lead / Finance Controller first. The CEO is a sponsor/escalation route, not automatically the best first recipient.
3. Personalise the first line using one verifiable business fact from the company's own website.
4. Ask for a 15-minute discovery call. Do not attach a large proposal on first contact.
5. If interested, qualify: number of sites/stores, SKU count, current inventory system, count frequency, transaction records, transfer process, known reconciliation pain and decision owner.
6. Offer a paid, tightly scoped pilot only after the scope and source-data availability are understood.
7. Follow up once after 4–5 business days; a second and final follow-up after another 5–7 business days. Record all touches and outcomes.

## Discovery questions

- How many stores, production sites or warehouses are in scope?
- What system or records hold the book balances?
- How are physical counts scheduled, witnessed and approved?
- How are transfers dispatched, received and matched?
- How are negative stock balances and post-cut-off transactions investigated?
- Who approves adjustments and signs off the reconciliation?
- What report does management receive, and how often?
- What would make a pilot successful, and who owns the budget?

## Commercial guardrails

- Do not offer free ongoing reconciliation. A short introductory call is not a free engagement.
- Do not quote a fixed fee until SKU volume, site count, data quality, count responsibility and deliverables are understood.
- Do not promise fraud detection, guaranteed accuracy, savings or loss prevention.
- Require an agreed scope, data-access permission, confidentiality terms, cut-off rules, client count evidence and approval responsibilities.
- Separate data preparation and analysis from client physical stock verification and adjustment approval.
- Do not upload prospect data to a public GitHub repository. Use synthetic data in the portfolio only.
