Management interpretation

Do not sign off this reconciliation as clean. The model identifies multiple issues requiring resolution: the stocktake cut-off, negative book balances, transfer pairing and an action linked to a pending count.

The absolute variance value is a prioritisation indicator, not automatically a loss figure. The signed net variance value is ₦2,434,328, while the absolute line-value total is ₦2,786,652. The difference reflects offsetting positive and negative line values.

Quantities span kg, litres, units, jerrycans, bottles and packs. A consolidated quantity total would be misleading. Any client implementation needs a validated UOM conversion policy, stock valuation method and documented source evidence.

How this becomes a client service

Inventory reconciliation and stock-control support: clean item/location masters; reconcile stock ledger to an agreed cut-off; structure physical-count results; identify exceptions; investigate transfers and negative stock; maintain corrective actions; and deliver a monthly management report.
